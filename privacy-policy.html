Privacy Policy

At Rent-a-Car, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and address.
  • Booking details, including car selection, pickup and drop-off dates, and locations.
  • Payment information required to process transactions (securely handled).
  • Website usage data through cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To process car rental bookings and manage your reservations.
  • To communicate important updates regarding your booking.
  • To improve our website and services based on user behavior.
  • For marketing communications, if you have opted-in.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with trusted service providers for:

  • Payment processing and financial transactions.
  • Fleet management and car delivery services.
  • Legal or regulatory compliance if required.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and improve our services. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal requirements.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time.
